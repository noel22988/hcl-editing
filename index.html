1. 成分多余 — Redundant Components (Extra Words)
句子中出现了不必要的成分，删去后句意仍然完整。常见于两个同义词并列使用的情况。The sentence contains unnecessary words that can be removed without changing the meaning. This usually happens when two synonyms are used together — pick one and drop the other.
a. 谓语多余 — Redundant Predicate (Are there two verbs with the same meaning?)
两个动词意思相同或高度相近，并列使用造成重复。只需保留其中一个动词即可。Two verbs with the same or very similar meaning are used back-to-back. Keep one, remove the other. Common pairs: 把握/控制, 讨论/商议, 钻研/探索.

2. 成分残缺 — Incomplete Components (Missing Parts)
句子缺少必要的成分，导致句意不完整或逻辑不清。改正时需补充缺失的成分，使句子结构完整。The sentence is missing a required part, making it grammatically incomplete or logically unclear. Fix it by adding the missing element.
a. 主语残缺 — Missing Subject (Who is doing the action?)
句子没有主语，读者不知道是谁在执行动作。常见于句首有“通过”“由于”“经过”等介词短语开头的情况。改正时需补充一个合适的主语。The sentence has no subject — we don't know WHO is doing the action. This often happens when the sentence starts with a phrase like 通过 (through), 由于 (due to), or 经过 (after). Fix: add a subject like 我们, 政府, 他 etc.

3. 搭配不当 — Improper Collocation (Wrong Word Pairing)
句子中某些成分之间的组合不符合汉语的表达习惯，使句意变得生硬或错误。改正时需将不搭配的词语替换为合适的词语。Certain words in the sentence don't naturally go together in Chinese — like saying "do a decision" instead of "make a decision" in English. The fix is to swap one word for a more natural partner.
a. 主谓搭配不当 — Subject-Verb Mismatch (Does the verb match the subject?) (Not tested yet)
主语和谓语之间的语义不匹配。即主语的性质与谓语动词所表达的动作或状态不相符。常见错误：用只能搭配人的动词来搭配事物，或用只能搭配具体事物的动词来搭配抽象概念。The subject and verb don't logically match — the action or state expressed by the verb doesn't fit the nature of the subject. Common mistake: using a verb that only applies to people for an abstract noun, or a verb for physical things applied to abstract concepts. Fix by swapping the verb for one that fits the subject.

b. 关联词搭配不当 — Wrong Conjunction Pairing (Do the connecting words go together?)

If you use the wrong partner word, the sentence sounds wrong. Common pairs to memorise: 虽然…但是, 不仅…而且 (not only…but also), 即使…也 (even if…still), 无论…都 (no matter…always), 不是…而是 (not…but).

4. 语序不当 — Improper Word Order (Wrong Sequence)
句子中词语或分句的排列顺序不符合汉语的逻辑或语法规则，导致句意混乱或表达不自然。改正时需调整词语或分句的位置。The words or phrases in the sentence are in the wrong order, making it sound unnatural or illogical. In Chinese, the typical order is: Subject + Time/Place + Verb + Object. Modifiers go before what they describe.
a. 主谓宾与修饰语语序不当 — Wrong Word Order (Are the words in the right position?)
句子中主语、谓语、宾语或修饰语的位置不正确。汉语基本语序：主语 + 状语 + 谓语 + 宾语，定语放在名词前，状语放在动词前。常见错误：时间/地点状语位置错误、谓语与宾语位置互换、修饰语与被修饰词顺序颠倒。The sentence components are in the wrong order. Key rules: (1) Time/place words go BEFORE the verb, not after. (2) Adverbs like 认真, 热烈, 不断 go BEFORE the verb they describe. (3) The verb comes BEFORE the object — don't swap them.

b. 分句语序不当 — Wrong Clause Order (Are the clauses in the right sequence?)
复句中各分句的排列顺序不符合逻辑或时间顺序。汉语复句中，分句通常遵循：① 时间先后顺序（先发生的事在前），② 因果逻辑（原因在前、结果在后，或结果在前再说明原因），③ 条件到结果的顺序。若分句顺序颠倒，会造成逻辑混乱。In a sentence with multiple clauses, the clauses are in the wrong order — like putting the result before the cause, or the conclusion before the evidence. Key rules: (1) Events should follow time order. (2) Cause before effect is the natural order, though Chinese sometimes states result first then explains. (3) Condition should come before consequence. Fix by reordering the clauses logically.

老师把重要的知识耐心地一遍又一遍讲了。
你的答案
正解
在「把」字句中，状语「耐心地一遍又一遍」应放在「把」之前。In a 把 sentence, the manner adverbs 耐心地一遍又一遍 go BEFORE 把.
第 9 题语序不当
这本书中国历史详细地介绍了。
你的答案
正解
宾语「中国历史」应在动词「介绍」后；状语「详细地」应在动词前。The object 中国历史 goes AFTER the verb; the adverb 详细地 goes BEFORE the verb.

正解
「热烈的」应紧贴「认可」。热烈的 is a modifier for 认可 — put it directly before 认可.

「非常」应在「出色」前。Degree adverb 非常 goes BEFORE the adjective 出色.

第 16 题语序不当

正解
「都」表全部，应放在动词「捐」前，不应插在「捐了」和「出去」之间。都 (all) goes BEFORE the verb 捐, not wedged between 捐了 and 出去.

正解

你的答案
正解
时间、地点状语应在动词前。Time (昨天) and place (从朋友那里) both go BEFORE the verb 收到.

正解
程度状语「深深地」应在动词前。深深地 (deeply) modifies the verb 感动 — move it before the verb.
